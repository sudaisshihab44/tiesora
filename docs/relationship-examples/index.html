Why some variants have no production screenshot

  • Paternal / Maternal Aunt and Uncle: the recorded aunt/uncle connectors in this family do not have a recorded gender, so the engine correctly falls back to Aunt/Uncle. The gender-specific labels are covered by the automated tests.
  • Verified half-sibling: both other parents are not recorded for the available sibling pairs. The gallery shows the honest Brother + Incomplete Family Records case instead.
  • Great-Aunt / Great-Uncle: no such pair exists in the currently connected production graph.
  • Invalid Selection: the Relationship Explorer only loads members of the active family, so two different families cannot be selected in the UI. The engine rejects them in the automated suite.
